# Wedding Planning Brief: Authentic Chinese Banquet with Modern City Pop Twists

> **Vision**: A fusion of 80s/90s-style Chinese restaurant wedding traditions with 1990s Japanese city pop influences, creating a warm, family-focused celebration that honors cultural heritage while embracing modern comfort and engagement.

---

## The Vision

A fusion of **80s/90s-style Chinese restaurant wedding traditions** with **1990s Japanese city pop influences**, replicating how immigrant families celebrated back then in bustling Cantonese banquet halls (e.g., US Chinatowns or suburbs like Monterey Park or Irvine). 

**The Focus**: Family, food, and culture—drawing from Hong Kong, Taiwanese, and Cantonese roots—but we're open to modern twists for comfort, engagement, and style, like neon accents, fusion elements, a retro lounge, and tech integrations.

**Key Point**: We've already booked and finalized the venue (a traditional Cantonese seafood restaurant in Orange County) and catering dinner (8-10 course banquet), so the planner's role is hands-on coordination for everything else.

**The Experience**: Imagine a lively evening where elders reminisce over symbolic dishes amid warm amber lights blending with teal/pink neon, kids play in a cozy nook, and young guests mingle with app updates and fusion cocktails—genuine warmth enhanced by modern romanticism.

**Photoshoot Studio**: Additionally, we need a dedicated photoshoot studio area inspired by Wong Kar-wai films: moody, cinematic retro HK 80s/90s look with hazy lighting, deep reds, neon accents, vintage props (e.g., old TVs, palms), and intimate poses for bridal party/couple shots.
