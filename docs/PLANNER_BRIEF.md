# Wedding Planning Brief: Authentic Chinese Banquet

## The Vision
80s/90s Chinese restaurant wedding - exactly how they were done then, with slight modern comfort updates (lounge area for second half mingling).

## Core Principles
- **Authentic, not stylized** - This is how our parents' generation did it
- **Restaurant-run** - They have the system, we use it
- **Food-centric** - The banquet IS the event
- **Natural flow** - No forced moments or rigid timeline

## The Venue
Chinese restaurant in Orange County with:
- Main dining hall for 80-100 guests (8-10 round tables)
- Stage for tea ceremony and speeches
- Secondary lounge/arcade space
- Existing décor (red curtains, gold accents, 囍 signs)
- Standard wedding package they've done hundreds of times

## The Program
```
6:00pm    Doors open, guests arrive
6:40pm    Grand entrance
6:55pm    Tea ceremony on stage (parents/elders only)
7:35pm    Toasts (one family, one friend - keep short!)
7:45pm    LOUNGE OPENS - guests can flow between spaces
8:15pm    Cake/champagne moment
8:45pm    Group photo on stage
9:00pm    Karaoke begins, free mingling
9:45pm    Final thanks
10:00pm   Service ends
```

## Your Role

### What You DO:
- Find/recommend the right Chinese restaurant
- Book their standard wedding package
- Coordinate ONE photographer (documentary style)
- Arrange bilingual MC if restaurant doesn't provide
- Setup basics: red envelope station, simple signage
- Day-of: Ensure smooth handoffs, then step back

### What You DON'T Do:
- Transform the venue
- Bring in elaborate décor
- Create complex timelines
- Coordinate multiple vendors
- Try to "elevate" or modernize
- Fight the restaurant's system

## Specific Requirements

### Must Have:
- Tea ceremony setup on stage
- 8-10 course Cantonese banquet
- Red envelope collection system
- Bilingual announcements (Mandarin/English)
- Natural documentation photography

### Nice to Have:
- Karaoke system (if venue doesn't have)
- Simple photo corner
- Minimal flowers for head table

### Absolutely NOT:
- Dance floor construction
- Elaborate lighting design
- Custom centerpieces
- Western wedding elements
- Instagram-focused styling

## Success Looks Like
- Elders commenting "This reminds me of weddings back home"
- Friends saying "I've never experienced anything like this"
- Natural moments captured, not staged ones
- Smooth flow that feels effortless
- Full bellies and warm memories

## Red Flags We're Avoiding
- Planners who want to "modernize" everything
- Over-styling or Instagram-focused approach
- Fighting against restaurant's proven system
- Suggesting unnecessary vendors or additions
- Not understanding Chinese banquet culture
