## Which Moodboard Images to Attach

Choose 3-4 that show:
1. **The venue vibe** - Round tables with lazy susans, warm lighting
2. **The ceremony** - Tea ceremony or couple in traditional/modern mix
3. **The aesthetic** - Candlelit tables or neon accents
4. **The energy** - Guests celebrating, natural moments

Caption the images simply:
- "Our venue style"
- "Tea ceremony inspo"  
- "Lighting mood"
- "Overall vibe"

## Key Changes from Original:

**Before:** 150+ words, lists of requirements  
**After:** 50-75 words, visual-first

**Before:** Explaining everything upfront  
**After:** Teasing the vibe, saving details for call

**Before:** Formal and detailed  
**After:** Conversational with emojis/bullets

**Before:** Text-heavy  
**After:** "Show don't tell" with images

---

## Pro Tips:

1. **Name your images smartly** - They'll see filenames
   - "chinese-banquet-tea-ceremony.jpg"
   - "candlelit-wedding-neon-accents.jpg"
   - "200-guest-seafood-restaurant.jpg"

2. **Make a collage** - One image with 4 panels shows everything instantly

3. **Test the preview** - Make sure images show in email preview

4. **Mobile-optimize** - Most planners check email on phones
