**Music/DJ**

### 2. Ceremony Coordination

**Tea Ceremony (6:55 PM - 7:10 PM)**
- Location: Main stage in banquet hall
- Setup: Chairs for elders, kneeling cushions, tea set
- Sequence: Serve parents first, then extended family
- Coordinate: Red envelope collection and thank you tracking
- Special: 3-minute video montage during ceremony

**Grand Entrance (6:40 PM)**
- Coordinate with MC for couple introduction
- Music: City pop remix or modern take on traditional song
- Route: From entrance through main hall to head table

### 3. Timeline Management

**Pre-Wedding Day:**
- Vendor confirmations 1 week prior
- Final headcount to restaurant 3 days prior
- Rehearsal at venue (if possible)

**Day-Of Schedule:**
- 3:30 PM - Vendor setup begins
- 5:15 PM - Couple arrives for photos
- 6:00 PM - Doors open for guests
- 6:40 PM - Grand entrance
- 6:45 PM - First course served
- 6:55 PM - Tea ceremony
- 7:45 PM - Lounge opens (coordinate transition)
- 8:45 PM - Group photo
- 12:30 AM - Event ends

### 4. Design Elements

**Overall Aesthetic:**
- Work WITH restaurant's existing red/gold decor
- Add subtle enhancements, not transformation
- Warm amber lighting in main hall
- Teal/pink neon accents in lounge area

**Specific Additions:**
- 300+ candles across all tables (battery operated OK)
- Neon 囍 sign behind ceremony stage
- Neon sign in lounge (Japanese/English text)
- Minimal floral enhancements
- No ceiling draping or major transformations

**Table Setup:**
- Restaurant handles: Lazy Susans, basic settings, standard centerpieces
- We add: Candles, place cards, favor boxes (if any)

### 5. Cultural Elements to Respect

**Must Understand:**
- Tea ceremony protocol and sequence
- Red envelope (hong bao) tradition
- Seating arrangements (family tables near front)
- Lucky colors and numbers
- Multiple toasts throughout dinner
- Table-to-table visits by couple

**Language Considerations:**
- Announcements in English and Cantonese
- Signage bilingual where appropriate
- Some elderly guests speak only Cantonese
