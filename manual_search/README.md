# Manual Wedding Planner Search Toolkit

## 📋 Quick Start Guide

This toolkit provides everything you need to manually find and hire the perfect wedding planner for your Chinese banquet wedding with city pop fusion elements. Total time investment: 8-10 hours over 2 weeks.

### 📁 File Directory

1. **`planner_tracking_spreadsheet.csv`** - Master tracking spreadsheet for all planners
2. **`search_strategy_guide.md`** - Step-by-step search instructions with exact queries
3. **`email_templates.md`** - Copy-paste email templates for outreach
4. **`planner_evaluation_scorecard.md`** - Scoring system to evaluate planners objectively
5. **`call_interview_questions.md`** - Complete interview guide for planner calls
6. **`final_comparison_matrix.md`** - Decision-making framework for final selection
7. **`planner_handoff_brief.md`** - Comprehensive brief to give your chosen planner

### 🚀 14-Day Action Plan

#### Days 1-3: Research Phase
- [ ] Open `search_strategy_guide.md` and follow search workflow
- [ ] Track all findings in `planner_tracking_spreadsheet.csv`
- [ ] Target: Find 15-20 potential planners

#### Days 4-6: Initial Outreach
- [ ] Use templates from `email_templates.md`
- [ ] Send personalized emails to top 10-12 planners
- [ ] Track responses in spreadsheet

#### Days 7-10: Evaluation & Calls
- [ ] Score responses using `planner_evaluation_scorecard.md`
- [ ] Schedule calls with top 3-5 planners
- [ ] Use `call_interview_questions.md` during calls

#### Days 11-13: Decision Making
- [ ] Complete `final_comparison_matrix.md` for top 3
- [ ] Check references
- [ ] Make final decision

#### Day 14: Hiring & Handoff
- [ ] Send contract to chosen planner
- [ ] Customize and send `planner_handoff_brief.md`
- [ ] Schedule kickoff meeting

### 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Use multiple browser tabs** - Open 10 planner profiles at once for faster scanning
2. **Set up email templates** - Save templates in Gmail for quick customization
3. **Book calls back-to-back** - Schedule all calls in one afternoon to maintain comparison freshness
4. **Take notes during calls** - Use the provided call notes template
5. **Trust your gut** - Chemistry matters as much as experience

### 🎯 Success Metrics

You'll know you've succeeded when:
- ✅ You have 3-5 strong planner candidates
- ✅ All have Chinese wedding experience
- ✅ At least 2 have worked at restaurant venues
- ✅ Pricing is within $3-5K budget
- ✅ You feel confident in your final choice

### ⏰ Time Investment Breakdown

- **Research:** 3-4 hours
- **Email outreach:** 1 hour
- **Email monitoring:** 30 min/day x 5 days = 2.5 hours
- **Phone calls:** 2-3 hours
- **Decision & contracting:** 1 hour
- **Total:** 8-10 hours

### 🔍 Key Search Priorities

Based on your wedding specifications:
1. **Must have Chinese/Asian wedding experience**
2. **Must understand restaurant venue logistics**
3. **Must be available for 3-4 month timeline**
4. **Should respect authentic approach over transformation**
5. **Bonus: Bilingual capabilities**

### 📞 Emergency Backup Plan

If you can't find suitable planners:
1. Expand search to Los Angeles (1 hour away)
2. Consider day-of coordinators + self-planning
3. Ask venue for their recommended planners
4. Check with Chinese community centers for referrals

### 🎊 Your Wedding Vision Reminder

**What you're looking for:** A planner who understands authentic 1980s-90s Chinese restaurant banquets and can add subtle modern touches without over-styling.

**What to avoid:** Planners who want to transform everything, have no cultural experience, or can't work within restaurant constraints.

## Need Help?

If you get stuck:
1. Start with the easiest searches (WeddingWire/The Knot)
2. Focus on quality over quantity (better to deeply research 10 than skim 30)
3. Don't hesitate to send follow-ups (planners are busy!)
4. Trust planners with specific examples over general claims
