## Search Workflow

### Day 1: Cast Wide Net (2 hours)
1. **WeddingWire** (30 min)
   - Search each query above
   - Look for "Featured" or "Award" badges
   - Check review count and ratings
   - Save 5-7 profiles

2. **The Knot** (30 min)
   - Use filters above
   - Sort by "Most Reviews"
   - Cross-reference with WeddingWire finds
   - Save 5-7 profiles

3. **Instagram** (45 min)
   - Search each hashtag
   - Look for recent posts (2024-2025)
   - Check tagged venues like @capitalseafood
   - Follow and save 8-10 profiles

4. **Google/Yelp** (15 min)
   - Quick searches for any missed local businesses
   - Check Google Business reviews
   - Add 3-5 more options

### Day 2: Deep Dive Research (1.5 hours)
For each saved planner:

1. **Portfolio Review** (3 min per planner)
   - Look for Chinese/Asian weddings
   - Check for restaurant venue experience
   - Note authentic vs over-styled approaches

2. **Social Proof** (2 min per planner)
   - Read 3-5 most recent reviews
   - Search for mentions of "Chinese," "tea ceremony," "bilingual"
   - Check response rate to reviews

3. **Availability Check** (1 min per planner)
   - Look for "2025 bookings open" posts
   - Check last posting date (active = good)
   - Note any "limited availability" warnings

### Day 3: Narrow and Organize (1 hour)
1. **Rank by Criteria**
   - Tier 1: Has Chinese wedding experience + available
   - Tier 2: Has Asian wedding experience + available
   - Tier 3: Multicultural experience + strong reviews

2. **Fill Tracking Spreadsheet**
   - Transfer top 15-20 to spreadsheet
   - Add all contact information
   - Note specific experience examples

- "Complete venue makeover"  
- "Pinterest-perfect"
- "Styled shoots only"
- "Minimum $10K budget"
- "12-month planning minimum"
- "Destination weddings only"

## Time-Saving Shortcuts

1. **Multi-tab browsing**: Open 10 profiles at once, quickly scan
2. **Bookmark folders**: Create "Tier 1," "Tier 2," "Maybe" folders
3. **Screenshot key info**: Grab pricing/availability quickly
4. **Use mobile voice notes**: Record quick thoughts while browsing
