## Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines That Work:
- "January 2026 Chinese Wedding - Coordinator Needed"
- "Quick Question About Your Availability"
- "Love Your Work at [Specific Venue]"
- "3-Month Timeline Wedding - Interested?"

### Send Times:
- Tuesday-Thursday
- 10am-12pm or 2pm-4pm
- Avoid Mondays and Fridays

### Follow-Up Schedule:
- Day 1: Initial email
- Day 3: Follow-up if no response
- Day 7: Final follow-up
- Day 8+: Try Instagram or phone

### Personalization Must-Haves:
- Reference specific work from portfolio
- Mention venue type experience
- Note any Chinese/Asian weddings they've done
- Comment on their review responses

### Keep It:
- Under 150 words
- Specific about timeline and budget
- Clear about what's already handled (venue/food)
- Warm but professional
