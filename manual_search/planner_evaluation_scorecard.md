# Wedding Planner Evaluation Scorecard

## Quick Scoring Guide (Total: 50 points possible)

### Section 1: Cultural Expertise (15 points)

**Chinese Wedding Experience (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: 5+ Chinese weddings, can name specific venues/traditions
- 4 points: 2-4 Chinese weddings with good examples
- 3 points: 1 Chinese wedding or extensive Asian wedding experience
- 2 points: General Asian/multicultural experience
- 1 point: Willing to learn but no direct experience
- 0 points: No relevant cultural experience

**Tea Ceremony Knowledge (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: Has coordinated multiple tea ceremonies, knows proper sequence
- 4 points: Has done tea ceremonies, understands basics
- 3 points: Witnessed/assisted with tea ceremonies
- 2 points: Researched and understands concept
- 1 point: Willing to learn
- 0 points: Unfamiliar and seems unconcerned

**Restaurant Venue Experience (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: Has worked at Capital Seafood or similar Chinese banquet halls
- 4 points: Multiple restaurant wedding experiences
- 3 points: Some restaurant venue experience
- 2 points: Understands constraints of restaurant venues
- 1 point: No experience but shows adaptability
- 0 points: Only works with traditional wedding venues

### Section 2: Practical Capabilities (15 points)

**Timeline Management (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: Enthusiastic about 3-4 month timeline, has done similar
- 4 points: Comfortable with short timeline, has systems in place
- 3 points: Can work with timeline but seems stressed
- 2 points: Prefers longer but willing to try
- 1 point: Very concerned about short timeline
- 0 points: Refuses or strongly discourages short timeline

**Vendor Network (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: Has Chinese-speaking vendors, photographers who know ceremonies
- 4 points: Strong vendor network, some with Asian wedding experience
- 3 points: Good general vendor network
- 2 points: Limited but quality vendor connections
- 1 point: Few vendor relationships
- 0 points: No established vendor network

**Communication Style (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: Prompt, clear, professional, culturally aware
- 4 points: Good communication, responsive
- 3 points: Adequate communication, some delays
- 2 points: Slow responses but thorough
- 1 point: Poor responsiveness or unclear
- 0 points: Very poor communication

### Section 3: Vision Alignment (10 points)

**Understanding of "Authentic + Modern" Balance (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: Gets it perfectly - respects tradition with subtle modern touches
- 4 points: Good understanding, asks right questions
- 3 points: Decent grasp but might need guidance
- 2 points: Leans too traditional or too modern
- 1 point: Misunderstands the vision
- 0 points: Wants to completely transform concept

**Design Aesthetic (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: Portfolio shows restrained elegance, works with existing decor
- 4 points: Clean aesthetic, not overdone
- 3 points: Some over-styling but generally good
- 2 points: Tends toward excessive decoration
- 1 point: Very different aesthetic vision
- 0 points: Only does elaborate transformations

### Section 4: Business Factors (10 points)

**Pricing Value (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: Under $3,500 with everything needed included
- 4 points: $3,500-4,000 with good value
- 3 points: $4,000-4,500 but negotiable
- 2 points: $4,500-5,000 at top of budget
- 1 point: Over $5,000 but willing to customize
- 0 points: Way over budget or hidden fees

**Availability & Responsiveness (0-5 points)**
- 5 points: Immediately available, responded within 24 hours
- 4 points: Available with quick response (1-2 days)
- 3 points: Available but slower response (3-4 days)
- 2 points: Mostly available, slow response
- 1 point: Limited availability or very slow
- 0 points: Unavailable or no response after follow-ups

## Red Flag Checklist (Any = Serious Concern)

□ Wants to completely change restaurant setup
□ No photos of actual Chinese/Asian weddings
□ Insists on 6+ month timeline minimum
□ Multiple bad reviews about communication
□ Seems unfamiliar with tea ceremony
□ Pushes expensive vendor packages
□ Portfolio only shows styled shoots
□ Dismissive of traditional elements
□ Not responsive to initial outreach
□ Tries to upsell unnecessary services

## Green Flag Checklist (More = Better)

□ Has specific Capital Seafood experience
□ Shows photos from actual Chinese banquets
□ Bilingual (English/Chinese) capabilities
□ Understands red envelope tradition
□ Has relationships with Chinese vendors
□ Comfortable with short timeline
□ Respects restaurant's existing setup
□ Mentions specific tea ceremony experience
□ Good reviews from Asian families
□ Responds within 24 hours
